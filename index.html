📄 Your Records & DVSA Retention

How Long We Keep Your Data

We follow DVSA guidance, keeping records 10% longer than the minimum required period for compliance protection.

✅ Daily Walkaround / Defect Reports
Kept for 16.5 months (DVSA min 15 months + 10%)
✅ Tachograph Data
Kept for 13.2 months (DVSA min 12 months + 10%)
✅ Licence / CPC / Medical Documents
Kept for 6.6 years (while employed + 12 months + 10%)
✅ Holiday / Employment Records
Kept for 6.6 years

What Happens When Records Expire?

Before any record is securely deleted at the end of its retention period:

  1. A copy is automatically emailed to your company's archive email address
  2. Your manager has 30 days to review or extend retention if needed
  3. After 30 days, the original record is permanently and securely deleted
